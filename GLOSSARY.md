# Glossary

## Site settings (Paramètres du site)

The values an administrator sets in the back office and the public site shows to every visitor. Today they are the contact email and the About text, which exists in French and in English; each can be changed without touching the others. There is exactly one set of site settings for the whole site, and it is never empty.

Not to be confused with a user's own profile or preferences (language, theme), which belong to that visitor.

## Expert (enseignant)

A teacher account, created by choosing "teacher" at registration. An expert signs in and browses like a student, and rates schools; rating programmes and courses (and, later, writing on the forum) is reserved for experts whose account has been approved. Not to be confused with a student, who has no approval step.

## Statut d'approbation

Where an expert account stands in the administrator's review: pending (the starting point), approved or rejected, with an optional reason when rejected. It only concerns experts, it is shown to the expert and to administrators, and a decision takes effect immediately without the expert signing in again. Experts who existed before the review was introduced are approved.

## Forum

The discussion space between students and experts. Anyone can read it; only students and approved experts write (a pending or rejected expert, and an administrator, cannot). It holds threads and their replies, in plain text only: what is typed is shown as typed, never interpreted as formatting or code. Writing is limited to 5 writes per minute per person: creating and editing threads and replies count together, deleting does not. Any signed-in student or expert can report a message; the administrator moderates (see Signalement and Masquer).

## Fil (thread)

A discussion opened in the forum: a title (150 characters at most) and an opening message (5000 at most), optionally about one school. Only its author edits or deletes it, deleting it removes its replies, and a locked thread refuses new replies (answer 409) while staying readable. Only an administrator locks or unlocks a thread (the lock and a reply being posted at the same moment never both win: a reply is accepted before the lock or refused after it). Listings show the newest first, 100 per page at most.

## Réponse (reply)

A message written under a thread (5000 characters at most). Replies are flat: one cannot answer another reply. Only its author edits or deletes it. Readers see the author's first name and whether they are a student or an expert, never an e-mail or a last name; when the author deleted his account the message stays and is shown as written by a deleted user.

## Signalement (report)

A user's alert about one forum message (a thread's opening message or a reply), with a reason of 255 characters at most. Any signed-in student or expert can report, whether or not the expert is approved; an administrator moderates instead and cannot report. One report per user and message, never on one's own message, and a hidden message cannot be reported (it is invisible). A report is open until the administrator closes it as handled or dismissed; closing it does not touch the message, and the first decision stands. Reports are personal data: only administrators read them, with the reporter's first name only. A report disappears with its message when the message is deleted, and stays, anonymous, when the reporter deletes his account.

## Masquer (hide)

The administrator's way to take a message out of the forum without destroying it: a hidden thread or reply is no longer returned to anyone but administrators (neither its text, nor in counts such as a thread's number of replies), its author can no longer edit or delete it, and it can be shown again. Hiding a thread hides its replies with it. Not to be confused with deleting, which is final and also removes the reports on the message.

## Session (appareil)

One device on which a user is signed in. A user can hold several sessions at once, each independent of the others. A session ends after seven days without activity on that device, and every session of the account ends together when its password changes, its e-mail address is confirmed or the account is deleted. Signing out in the browser only forgets the session on that device; it does not end it.
_Avoid_: token, connexion, login
