# Glossary

## Site settings (Paramètres du site)

The values an administrator sets in the back office and the public site shows to every visitor. Today they are the contact email and the About text, which exists in French and in English; each can be changed without touching the others. There is exactly one set of site settings for the whole site, and it is never empty.

Not to be confused with a user's own profile or preferences (language, theme), which belong to that visitor.

## Expert (enseignant)

A teacher account, created by choosing "teacher" at registration. An expert signs in and browses like a student, and rates schools; rating programmes and courses (and, later, writing on the forum) is reserved for experts whose account has been approved. Not to be confused with a student, who has no approval step.

## Statut d'approbation

Where an expert account stands in the administrator's review: pending (the starting point), approved or rejected, with an optional reason when rejected. It only concerns experts, it is shown to the expert and to administrators, and a decision takes effect immediately without the expert signing in again. Experts who existed before the review was introduced are approved.

## Forum

The discussion space between students and experts. Anyone can read it; only students and approved experts write (a pending or rejected expert, and an administrator, cannot). It holds threads and their replies, in plain text only: what is typed is shown as typed, never interpreted as formatting or code. Writing is limited to 5 writes per minute per person: creating and editing threads and replies count together, deleting does not. Moderation (reports, hiding) is a later slice.

## Fil (thread)

A discussion opened in the forum: a title (150 characters at most) and an opening message (5000 at most), optionally about one school. Only its author edits or deletes it, deleting it removes its replies, and a locked thread refuses new replies (answer 409) while staying readable; who can lock a thread belongs to the moderation slice, which comes later. Listings show the newest first, 100 per page at most.

## Réponse (reply)

A message written under a thread (5000 characters at most). Replies are flat: one cannot answer another reply. Only its author edits or deletes it. Readers see the author's first name and whether they are a student or an expert, never an e-mail or a last name; when the author deleted his account the message stays and is shown as written by a deleted user.
