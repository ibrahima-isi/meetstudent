# Glossary

## Site settings (Paramètres du site)

The values an administrator sets in the back office and the public site shows to every visitor. Today they are the contact email and the About text, which exists in French and in English; each can be changed without touching the others. There is exactly one set of site settings for the whole site, and it is never empty.

Not to be confused with a user's own profile or preferences (language, theme), which belong to that visitor.

## Expert (enseignant)

A teacher account, created by choosing "teacher" at registration. An expert signs in and browses like a student, and rates schools; rating programmes and courses (and, later, writing on the forum) is reserved for experts whose account has been approved. Not to be confused with a student, who has no approval step.

## Statut d'approbation

Where an expert account stands in the administrator's review: pending (the starting point), approved or rejected, with an optional reason when rejected. It only concerns experts, it is shown to the expert and to administrators, and a decision takes effect immediately without the expert signing in again. Experts who existed before the review was introduced are approved.

## Forum

The future discussion space between students and experts. Its scope (who can post, who moderates) is not defined yet; until then it appears in the navigation of the public site and the back office as a disabled "coming soon" entry.
